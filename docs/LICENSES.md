# Licenses and asset notes

Not legal advice - a summary of what each piece's own license says, checked on 2026-10-09
against the installed versions. Running V1 costs nothing; the notes below matter if the use
grows (a company) or the software is distributed or sold.

## Made in this project (no third-party rights)

| What | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mascot and all artwork (PNG) | `assets/mascot`, `assets/exercises`, `assets/anatomy`, `assets/props`, `assets/icons` (originals in `assets/incoming`) | The channel owner's own character; the images were generated by the owner with image tools (prompts: `docs/ART_PROMPTS_READY.md`). Usage rights follow that tool's terms for the owner's account - check them once before monetizing. |
| Music loops and sound effects | `assets/music`, `assets/sfx` | Synthesized by `tools/audio/generate_audio_assets.py` - no samples, no recordings. |
| Code | `src/`, `renderer/src`, `web/`, `tools/` | This project's own. |

## Content

* Scripts are written by the local model for each video and checked for near-duplicates
  against earlier videos; the human review checklist asks for accuracy before publishing.
* No commercial songs, no stock footage, no images of real people.
* Nothing is uploaded automatically; publishing (and any platform's terms) is the user's step.
